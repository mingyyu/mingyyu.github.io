About

I’m an undergraduate in Singapore who likes building things other people can actually open and use.

Most of what’s on this page started as a question — can a library system handle real loans, can an entire empire game run inside a chat window — and turned into something that shipped. The parts I enjoy most are the unglamorous ones: schema design, getting state right, making the thing not break when someone else touches it.

Right now I’m going deeper on Go and Postgres, and pointing myself at AI — I think that’s where the interesting work is going to be. The unglamorous parts still decide whether it works: what data the model actually sees, what state it keeps between turns, whether someone trusts an answer enough to act on it. That’s the part I want to build.
